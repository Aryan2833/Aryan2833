# Aryan Patel

I build things I care about — usually because I wanted them to exist and they didn't.

My background is in Computer Science, but what stuck wasn't the syntax. It was the way of thinking: break a problem down, find the pattern, build something that makes it better. These days that shows up in games, privacy tools, and data projects that try to be useful to someone other than me.

This is where I experiment, ship, and occasionally make something worth sharing.

---

### 🛠 What I build

**Games** — Retro-inspired, built from scratch, actually playable.
**Privacy tools** — Helping people see and control their digital footprint.
**Community data** — Turning scattered public data into something that drives local action.

---

### 📌 Projects

**[Retro Rush](https://github.com/Aryan2833/retro-rush)** — A browser-based platformer inspired by Nokia-era classics. Three worlds, no dependencies, built with vanilla JS. → [Play it](https://aryan2833.github.io/retro-rush/)

**GreenPulse Ahmedabad** — A tool that helps NGOs find where tree plantation will have the most environmental impact, using live air quality and satellite data. *(in progress)*

**Phantom** — A digital footprint scanner: check breaches, find your accounts across 60+ platforms, get a prioritized cleanup plan. *(in progress)*

---

### What I care about

Good tools disappear into the problem they solve. I care about building things that are genuinely useful, that respect the people using them, and that make some small corner of the world work a little better.
